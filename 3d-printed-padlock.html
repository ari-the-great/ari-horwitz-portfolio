3D Printed Padlock

A volunteer project for Saint Louis University's (SLU) Stem Immersion Camp. The goal was to teach talented high school students about the applications of engineering.

The Padlock

Teaching

Design Inspiration

I created these fully 3D printed padlocks which were designed to allow users to see the entire lock mechanism. I included linear TPU springs (compliant mechanisms) to simulate the feel and function of a real lock.

After presenting the lock to the students, they got to get their hands on and attempt to pick them. I guided them through the activity while explaining how the components work.

The design was influenced by existing clear padlocks for teaching lockpicking. Although good educational tools, these locks were too difficult to pick for my desired activity.
