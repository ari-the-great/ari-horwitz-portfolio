4 Degree of Freedom Drawing Robot

For Medical Robotics at Georgia tech, we created a 4 DOF desktop robot that can draw a desired shape on any given axis. The design consists of 4 high-torque stepper motors with encoders, bearings, and couplers to allow for smooth motion and control. 

I was the mechanical engineer lead on the project and heavily involved in:

- Designing and 3D printing each of the links

- Circuit design and wiring

- Inverse kinematics based feedback control via a resolved rates algorithm that allows the end effector to follow a preprogrammed trajectory
