Concussion Monitoring Baseball Helmet

An innovative, cheap solution to display force data for helmets used in sports like baseball and football. 

Ansys Simulation and Modeling

1st Place Design Award

Battery and Arduino Casing

To aid the placement of the force sensors on the helmet, I lead the teams use of Ansys to simulate the dynamic stress distribution from a ball impact.

For the Medical Design Club (Cal Poly) design challenge 2023, our project won 1st place.

My other role for the project was to design the housing apparatus for the battery, Arduino, and led lights. The casing had to match the curve of the helmet, be durable for impact protection, and be easily accessible for in game repairs.
